How We Help

From your first message to the day your case closes, this is how Shadow Garden works.

  1. You submit your case

    Tell us what is happening through the case form. Share only what you are comfortable sharing. You can add more later.

  2. Shadow reads it personally

    Every case is reviewed by the founder. No assistants and no outside readers. We decide if we can take it, and we tell you honestly.

  3. We agree on terms in private

    Our work is paid. The scope and the price are discussed privately with you before anything begins. Nothing starts without your agreement.

  4. One member is assigned

    A single member takes your file. No one else can open it. You will not learn who they are, and that is part of keeping you safe.

  5. We act, and we stay

    Protection, defence or escape, depending on your case. We keep you updated and stay with you until the danger is gone.

  6. Your case closes, your file goes

    When the case ends, your file is sealed and removed, except where the law requires us to keep something.

What stays the same

  • You choose how much to share. We never ask for more than the case needs.
  • No one can see your file except the assigned member and Shadow.
  • We are honest. If we cannot help, we say so early.

Start with one message

You do not have to figure everything out first. Just tell us what is happening.
