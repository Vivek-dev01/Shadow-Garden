Questions, Answered

The things people ask before they trust us with a case.

Who can submit a case?

Anyone whose safety is at risk. We mainly protect women and public figures such as idols, actors, models and creators, but we also listen to anyone else who has nowhere else to turn.

What kinds of cases do you take?

Threats on your life, harassment, cyber attacks, blackmail, extortion and escape from abuse. If your case is something else, send it anyway. Shadow decides after reading it.

How much does it cost?

Our work is paid, and the price depends on your case. We discuss it privately with you before anything starts. Nothing begins without your agreement.

Who will see my case?

Only the member assigned to you and Shadow, the founder. No one else can open your file.

Will I know who is protecting me?

No. Our members have no names and no faces. That is how both you and they stay safe.

Will anyone know I contacted you?

We keep every case private. To help, use a safe email and a device only you control. Never write to us from a device someone else can read.

What if you cannot help me?

We will tell you early and honestly. We never promise what we cannot deliver.

How long until I hear back?

Shadow reads every case personally, so the time varies. Mark the case as urgent in the form if it is happening now. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency services first.

What happens to my file when the case ends?

When your case closes, the file is sealed and removed, except where the law requires us to keep something.

Is Shadow Garden real?

No. Shadow Garden is a fictional project. It is not a real organization and cannot help with real cases. If you need help, please contact your local emergency services or a trusted support organization.

Still have a question?

Write to us. Shadow reads every message personally.
