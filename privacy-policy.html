Privacy Policy

What we collect, who can see it, how long we keep it, and where our promises end.

Shadow Garden is a fictional project. This page describes how the organization would handle information in the story. No real data is collected on this website.

What we collect

Only what a case needs: an alias, a safe way to reach you, and what you tell us about your situation. We do not ask for more than the case requires, and you choose how much to share.

Who can see it

Your file is seen by the member assigned to your case and by Shadow, the founder. No one else can open it. Members never learn more about you than their work needs.

How we use it

Your information is used for one purpose: to review and handle your case. We do not sell it, trade it or use it for marketing.

How long we keep it

When your case closes, the file is sealed and removed. If a law requires us to keep something, we keep only that, only for as long as the law demands.

How we protect it

Access to files is limited to the people named above. Our future systems are planned with encrypted connections, controlled access and activity records. No system is perfect, so we also ask you to take care on your side.

Where our promises end

We take your privacy very seriously, but we cannot promise absolute secrecy or guaranteed safety. We may be forced to disclose information where the law requires it, or where someone’s life is in immediate danger. We will tell you honestly if that situation ever arises.

What you can do

  • Use a safe email and a device only you control.
  • Share only what you are comfortable sharing.
  • Ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to remove it.
  • Clear your browser history if someone else uses your device.

Questions

If something here is unclear, write to us through the contact page. Please do not send sensitive details in a general message.
