Who We Help

Fame, money or silence can make a person a target. We stand with those who have nowhere else to turn.

People we protect

Our work began with women, and they remain our first concern. But danger does not choose its victims by title, so neither do we. If your life is at risk and no one else will act, you can bring your case to us.

Idols & Entertainers

Idols, actors, singers and models whose fame brings obsessive fans, stalkers and threats.

Creators & Public Voices

Streamers, influencers and writers targeted by harassment, leaks and organized attacks.

Executives & Businesswomen

Women in power who face intimidation, blackmail and pressure meant to push them out.

The Wealthy & the Watched

People whose money makes them targets for extortion, kidnapping threats and stalking.

People Trapped in Abuse

Victims of domestic violence who cannot leave safely on their own.

Anyone Else in Danger

Ordinary people with life-threatening cases that the system cannot or will not handle.

Who we cannot work for

  • Anyone who wants to hurt someone else. We protect people. We do not hunt them.
  • Revenge dressed up as a case. If the story does not hold, Shadow will see it.
  • Anyone who lies to us. A false case puts real members at risk and ends the work.
