Case Studies

A few files from the garden. Names, places and details have been changed. The outcomes have not.

Every story on this page is fictional. They show the kind of cases Shadow Garden takes, not real people or real events.

The Idol and the Watcher

Stalking & threats on a public figure

The situation. A young idol began finding notes in places no stranger should reach. Her management had no answers, and the police had no case to open.

What we did. One member was assigned. Shadow reviewed her schedule, her circle and her routes, and a quiet watch began around her.

The outcome. The source was found and the contact stopped. She performed again within the month, and never knew who had stood behind her.

The Executive and the Photograph

Blackmail & extortion

The situation. A businesswoman received a message demanding money to keep a private photograph from her board. She had already paid once, and the demands kept growing.

What we did. We stopped her from paying again, secured her accounts and dealt with the blackmailer directly.

The outcome. The demands ended and the photograph never surfaced. She kept her company and her name.

The Creator and the Leak

Cyber defence & harassment

The situation. A popular creator’s private messages and address were posted online. Strangers began arriving at her gate.

What we did. Her accounts were locked down, the spread of the leak was contained and the people behind it were traced.

The outcome. The posts came down and the attackers backed off. She moved to a new address that no one knows.

The Mother Who Left

Escape from abuse

The situation. A woman had lived for years in fear. Every time she tried to leave, she was found and brought back.

What we did. A private exit was planned over several weeks. She and her child left in one night to a new city and a new life.

The outcome. She was never found. Today she works, her child goes to school, and her old life cannot reach her.
