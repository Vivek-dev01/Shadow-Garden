Engagement

Our work is paid, and the terms are private. Here is how an engagement begins, and what you can expect from us.

Why we charge

Protection costs time, skill and risk. Charging for our work keeps the garden running and keeps every member committed. It also means we answer to you, and to no one who could use your case against you.

We do not publish a price list. Every case is different, so every engagement is agreed with you directly.

How an engagement begins

  1. Review, free of charge

    Shadow reads your case first. There is no fee for the review, and no obligation after it.

  2. A private offer

    If we accept, you receive the scope of work and the price privately. Nothing is shared with anyone else.

  3. Your decision

    Take your time. You can accept, ask questions or walk away. Nothing begins until you agree.

  4. Work starts

    One member is assigned to you, and your case moves from paper to action.

What shapes the price

How serious the danger is

A stalker and a death threat are not the same work, and they are not priced the same.

How long it may take

Some cases end in days. Others need months. We stay as long as it takes.

How hard it is to reach

Powerful opponents and cases across borders cost more than a single, nearby threat.

What we never do

  • Charge for the review. Reading your case is always free.
  • Raise the price in secret. If the scope changes, you hear it from us first.
  • Pressure you to decide. A rushed yes is not a real yes.
  • Sell or share your details. Payment information is treated like the case itself.
