Who we are

Shadow Garden is a private organization that answers to no one but its own code. We protect people facing threats the law cannot reach in time: idols and celebrities who are hunted, women who are trapped, and anyone whose life has been put at risk.

Our members have no names and no faces. Only one person sees every file from start to finish: Shadow, the founder.

What we handle

Threats on Your Life

Death threats, stalkers and watchers. We put a quiet wall between you and the danger.

Harassment & Intimidation

Public figures and private people alike. If someone is making your life small, we end it.

Cyber Defence

Leaks, hacks and digital attacks. We lock the doors and trace who is knocking.

Blackmail & Extortion

When someone holds your secrets over you, we take that power away from them.

Escape from Abuse

Domestic violence victims get a way out, a new place to stand, and people who stay.

The Cases No One Takes

Our code

  • Your case stays yours. Only the assigned member and Shadow ever see it.
  • You never learn who protects you. That is how you stay safe.
  • A case, once taken, is finished. No matter who stands in the way.
  • We charge for our work. Silence, protection and risk all have a price.
