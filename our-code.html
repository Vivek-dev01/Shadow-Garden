Our Code

We answer to no one but this. Every member keeps it, and Shadow enforces it.

Your case stays yours

Only the assigned member and Shadow ever see your file. No one else can open it, not even other members.

No names, no faces

You will never know who protects you. Our members never learn more about you than the case needs. That is how both sides stay safe.

A case taken is a case finished

We do not walk away halfway. When we accept your case, we stay until you are safe, whoever stands in the way.

We never use our power for ourselves

We act for the people we protect and never for personal gain. No member profits from a client's pain.

Our work has a price

Silence, protection and risk are not free. Terms are agreed privately and honestly before anything begins.

We tell the truth

If we cannot help, we say so early. We never promise what we cannot deliver.

Betrayal has no second chance

A member who leaks a case, sells a name or breaks the code is gone. Shadow's decision is final.

Trust us with your case

This code is the only promise we make. We keep it.
